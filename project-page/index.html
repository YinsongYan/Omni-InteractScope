Overview

Omni-InteractScope evaluates assistants that follow continuous video and audio streams and respond at useful moments. Each interaction combines a stream with user context in text, speech, or images. Responses may be answers, alerts, warnings, tool calls, or successive guidance; the benchmark evaluates their content, timing, and coverage.

The benchmark contains 1,479 interactions and 1,790 response targets across eight task groups. T1–T7 use online videos spanning indoor, outdoor, and digital-screen scenes to test explicit requests. T8 uses self-recorded first-person videos for visual and hearing assistance, including reminders without an event-specific question, audiovisual questions, and multi-step guidance.

Tasks

Tasks T1–T7 begin with an explicit request. T8 covers assistance through reminders, audiovisual questions, and step-by-step guidance.

T1Tool invocationInvoke a tool when the requested event occurs.
T2Event notificationNotify the user when a specified event appears.
T3Risk and error interjectionIntervene when a hazard or mistake becomes apparent.
T4Cumulative-state trackingFollow a count or state as it changes over time.
T5Temporal conditionsRespond when a condition about time is met.
T6Nonverbal and workflow cuesUse gestures and context to infer when help is needed.
T7Continuous responsesGive multiple timely responses as events unfold.
T8AssistanceProvide reminders (A), answer audiovisual questions (B), or guide a multi-step task (C).

Examples

These are recordings and reference annotations from the benchmark. Each excerpt begins at 0:00, so the timestamps below match the video player.

T8-A · Query-free reminder

A group of chickens moves onto the park walkway. Without waiting for an event-specific question, the assistant should warn the user before they reach the birds.

26.8 s · “There are chickens on the path ahead. Slow down and walk around them.”

T8-B · Audiovisual question

User: “Did this black-and-white bird chirp?” Bird calls are audible while several birds are visible, but the sound cannot be attributed to the bird in question.

5.7 s · “I hear birds chirping, but the white bird with black spots is feeding, so I cannot tell whether it made the sound.”

T8-C · Multi-step guidance

User: “I need to use a single-journey ticket to enter the metro station through the entry gate.” The assistant guides the user through the gate in three steps.

  1. 3.3 s · Walk toward the entry gates ahead.
  2. 8.9 s · Scan the ticket on the reader to the right.
  3. 11.9 s · After the successful scan, pass through the gate.
