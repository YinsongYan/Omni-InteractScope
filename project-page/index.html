Overview

Omni-InteractScope studies assistants that observe a continuous video and audio stream and respond when something in the scene calls for it. The benchmark tests both what an assistant says or does and when it acts. Requests may depend on an event, a changing state, a temporal condition, or a sequence of steps; some assistance scenarios begin without an explicit request.

The benchmark contains 1,479 interactions and 1,790 annotated response targets across eight task groups. It draws on online video and first-person recordings from everyday activities. Each target specifies the expected response and its valid time window.

Tasks

Tasks T1–T7 begin with an explicit request. T8 covers assistance through reminders, audiovisual questions, and step-by-step guidance.

T1Tool invocationInvoke a tool when the requested event occurs.
T2Event notificationNotify the user when a specified event appears.
T3Risk and error interjectionIntervene when a hazard or mistake becomes apparent.
T4Cumulative-state trackingFollow a count or state as it changes over time.
T5Temporal conditionsRespond when a condition about time is met.
T6Nonverbal and workflow cuesUse gestures and context to infer when help is needed.
T7Continuous responsesGive multiple timely responses as events unfold.
T8AssistanceProvide reminders (A), answer audiovisual questions (B), or guide a multi-step task (C).

T8-B · Audiovisual question

User: “Are the chicks in front of me making any sounds?” The answer depends on both the visible chicks and the scene audio.

15.0 s · “The chicks are making chirping sounds.”

T8-C · Multi-step guidance

User: “Help me take the elevator to floor 7, and remind me to get out when we arrive.” The assistant must respond at three different moments.

  1. 32.0 s · Point out the floor 7 button.
  2. 58.0 s · Prepare the user to leave as floor 7 approaches.
  3. 63.5 s · Tell the user that the doors are open and guide them out.
